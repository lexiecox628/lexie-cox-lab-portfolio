# A8 – Design something useful

## Reading and Writing

Asking smarter questions matters for many reasons. In the design process you are constantly asking questions and it is important to ask the right ones to go down the right path. Asking smarter questions clarify things, encourage further thought, and overall give you a good basis on the problem you are trying to solve and the overall design. If you are working in a group it also allows everyone to be on the same page, more than likely someone has the same question and it is important to clarify. While asking questions is good, asking smarter questions is what makes a mediocre concept into a great design.

## Problem Statement

I decided to design something that will help people that struggle with everyday things that seem simple to most people. I found out that one of the main things people with dexterity issues deal with is not being able to open bottles or cans on their own. I looked up products that were already made to help with this and made it my own way. The purpose of my design is to make opening twist top bottles and pull tab cans easier to open. My mother has a condition that affects the dexterity in her hands to the point where it is really hard to even hold things let alone open stuff without help. I am designing this product for people such as my mom who struggle with opening tabs and twist tops without help. This allows them to have more independence. My approach to my design is to create something that has more leverage to make it easier to twist off the top and lift the tab. I also wanted to put into consideration that my design needs to be easy to grab.

## Lessons Learned

Some of the lessons I learned is how everyday things can be hard for people who have dexterity issues. Even though my mom lives with it I never knew how much she struggled to do certain things. However, I know that there are things that we can create to help people such as my mom. 

For 3D printing I learned how to add rivets into a design but I probably did it the long and difficult way. One thing I would change about my design is the fact that it is a little difficult to get the bottle cap back on because it is getting pressed together. Therefore, I would make the inside diameter just a tad bit bigger so it doesn't press it together as much. 

Overall, this project took me 10 hours between the research, design, and printing.
