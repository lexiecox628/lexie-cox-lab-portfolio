The objective of this assignment is to research different linkage mechanisms by how they work, and there purpose. We are then suppose to create a design based on our research. 

## Research

One recent patent of a linkage of mechanism is a compliant four-bar-based universal joint. A basic four-bar has a base that doesn't move, the driving part that gets power, a middle part that connects the driving part to the part that is moving up and down. A basic four-bar is made to the part moving up and down moves parallel to the ground. The compliant four-bar-based universal joint is a single-piece that uses torque and changes the angle between the input and output shafts. This uses rigid parts instead of pin joints like in a basic four-bar. These rigid parts are elastic hinges that fold instead pins that just hold parts together. Some industrial applications is steering in a vehicle. It is used in compact steering columns to reduce the vehicles weight and get rid of assembly parts.

Another recent patent that involves linkages is the scissor lift with middle pin offset and kicker. This patent was granted in 2026 and the patent number is 12428277. A traditional scissor lift is crisscrossing material that stacks on top of each other that moves vertically. It is powered by something that pushes the bottom stack together and apart to move it up and down. Since there is a lot of pressure when the lift is fully down since the material is all stacked it takes a lot of force to push the lift back up. However, the new patent offsets the middle pivot pin axis to make the angle easier to propel the lift up. It is useful for lifts that raise up heavy objects such as a car lift.

## Design

For this assignment I chose to design my own grabber using the linkage of a scissor lift design. I chose this because I thought it would be interesting for me to learn more about pins and how moveable parts work. I also wanted something that would be practical and useful. My entire design is 3D printed. 

Pins: The pins are what holds the whole thing together and allows it to more. I decided to print my pins because I have never printed functional pins and I thought it would be very useful to know. 

Handles: The handle of the design is based off of a scissor handle. It is meant to be easy to hold and control the grabber. These are attached to the body of the grabber which means it is not a movable part but it will control the whole thing. 

Body: The body of the design acts as the anchor of the whole design. It is where the linkage mechanism is as well. The linkage allows for the grabber to extend out to grab things further away. 

Claw: The claw is the part that is actually holding onto an object. When the mechanism is contracted the claw is open. However, when the linkage extends out the claw also closes.

## Lessons Learned

Overall this assignment took me 9 hours. The research took me an hour to 1.5 hours to complete. The design took me 5 hours total with all of the revisions and constraints. The printing took 2 hours total with the 4 different prints. With the total assembly and testing taking 30 min. 

My biggest mistake in this process was not adding tolerances to the pins during my first print. This set me back because I wasn't able to move forward with the process. After adding the tolerances my pins worked perfectly. I also made the mistake of putting the pins too close together when printing meaning that it got sloppy. This was a waste of material and time. After moving the pins further apart and printing fewer it allowed the pins to fit again.
