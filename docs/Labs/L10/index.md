# L11- Snap Fit Design for SLA Printing

## Objective

In this lab we are suppose to create a design with a snap fit using the SLA printer

## Research and Modeling Principles

On this project, I used a cantilever snap fit, which must withstand temporary elastic strain to lock securely into a snug spot. To take the parts apart, the beam has to bend and strain once again to release. Because of this, cantilever beams require the right ratios of length, width, and thickness so they do not snap during assembly or disassembly.

Since SLA resin is much more brittle than the PLA we used previously, it does not bend as easily and is stiffer to move. Keeping this material difference in mind is critical so the beam does not exceed its elastic limit and fracture. To account for the lower flexibility of SLA, I need to design the cantilever beam to be longer and thinner, allowing it to deflect enough to clear the catch without breaking or permanently deforming.
