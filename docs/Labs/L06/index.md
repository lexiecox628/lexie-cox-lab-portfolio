The purpose of this assignment is to create a snap fit that attaches to a part that was chosen.

## Design

The first thing that I did after picking my part was make any measurements I may need using a caliper. My measurements are as follows - Diameter of the holes: 0.118 in, Thickness: 0.06 in, Length: 1.397 in, and width: 1.24 in. These measurements will be useful when designing my snap fit.
